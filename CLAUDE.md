This project's rules live in files under `.agents/`:

- [.agents/DEV_RULES.md](.agents/DEV_RULES.md) — generic engineering principles (any project, any language).
- [.agents/SWIFT_LANG_RULES.md](.agents/SWIFT_LANG_RULES.md) — generic Swift/SwiftUI/AppKit practices (any Swift project).
- [.agents/WILES_RULES.md](.agents/WILES_RULES.md) — architecture, release process, and workflow specific to the Wiles app.
- [.agents/WILES_UI_UX_RULES.md](.agents/WILES_UI_UX_RULES.md) — Wiles' own UI/UX design and interaction conventions.
- [.agents/GENERAL_RULES.md](.agents/GENERAL_RULES.md) — general collaboration rules (not code-specific).

Read all five before writing or reviewing any code in this repository. Their rules are not suggestions — follow them exactly, including the response-language and commit-discipline rules in `WILES_RULES.md`.
